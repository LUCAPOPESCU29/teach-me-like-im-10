# Indie Hackers Launch Post

**Title:**
I built a free learning app and kept adding features for 6 months. Here's everything I learned.

**Body:**

## What it is

Teach Me Like I'm 10 (teachmelikeim10.xyz) — type any topic, get it explained across 5 levels of depth. Level 1 is for a curious kid. Level 5 is real.

No ads. No paywall. No account required.

---

## Why I built it

I kept googling things and getting stuck between two bad options:

1. Wikipedia — accurate but assumes a lot of prior knowledge
2. Kids' explainer sites — patronizing and too shallow

The 5-level structure was the insight. You pick your depth. The same topic can be explained simply or in full detail — different people need different entry points.

---

## What I added over 6 months

Started with the core (type → 5 levels). Then I kept using it myself and kept building.

**Modes:**
- Quiz Battle — two topics go head-to-head in a generated quiz
- Speed Run — race to Level 5 with a timer
- Spot Errors — it plants deliberate mistakes, you find them
- Debate — structured both-sides argument on any topic
- Time Machine — explain a modern concept to someone in the past
- Playground — interactive formula sandbox
- Compare — side-by-side concept comparisons

**Gamification:**
- XP earned from every action (topics, quizzes, daily challenges, streaks)
- Streak system with a freeze shop (buy freezes with XP, use them when you miss a day)
- Global leaderboard
- Titles and badges earned through milestones
- XP Shop — perks like +50% permanent XP multiplier, unlimited streak freezes, blackjack hints

**Social:**
- Friends and following system
- Study Rooms (collaborative sessions)
- Public profiles with learning DNA fingerprint

**Progress:**
- Solar System visualization — learned topics become planets in orbit
- Concept Map — shows how your topics connect
- XP history charts

---

## What's actually working

**Streaks + XP is stronger than I expected.** The combination of daily login rewards, streak protection, and XP stakes creates a genuine habit loop. People check back every day not because I emailed them — because they don't want to lose their streak.

**Gamification brings people back; explanation quality brings them in the first place.** The product needs to earn trust on first use. The game layer keeps them.

**Blackjack was a joke feature that turned real.** I added it for fun and it became one of the most-used features. There's something about wagering XP you *earned from studying* that creates a weird emotional attachment to not losing it.

---

## What I'm figuring out

**Growth.** I'm decent at building, not great at marketing. This post is part of that effort.

**Monetization.** Currently zero revenue. The app is free with no plans to paywall the core experience. I'm thinking: premium perks, team/classroom accounts, or a "supporter" tier. Not sure yet.

**Scope.** Every week I think of 3 more features. Saying no is hard when you're the only decision-maker.

## Tech stack

Next.js 16 (App Router, Turbopack) + Supabase + Vercel. Full mobile PWA. Framer Motion for animations.

---

## What I'd do differently

Launched the core and added features without validating what people actually wanted. I should have talked to users after week 2, not month 4.
