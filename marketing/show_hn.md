**Title:**
Show HN: Teach Me Like I'm 10 – Explain any topic across 5 levels of depth

**URL:** teachmelikeim10.xyz

**Comment (post immediately after submitting):**

Type any topic — physics, history, finance, biology — and get a 5-level explanation ranging from genuinely simple (Level 1, written for a curious kid) to meaningfully complex (Level 5, assumes you can handle real detail).

No account required. Works immediately.

What I built around the core:

**Modes:**
- Quiz Battle: two topics fight each other through generated questions
- Speed Run: race to Level 5, timed and scored
- Spot Errors: I plant deliberate mistakes in an explanation, you identify them
- Debate: structured both-sides argument on any topic
- Time Machine: explain a modern concept to a historical audience

**Gamification:**
- XP system with level progression and titles
- Streak tracking with a freeze shop (miss a day, lose your streak — unless you banked a freeze)
- Leaderboard
- XP Shop with cosmetics and permanent perks (e.g., Frost Titan = unlimited freezes for 50k XP, Golden Touch = +50% all XP for 25k XP)
- Blackjack where you bet earned XP

**Progress:**
- Solar System visualization — each topic you've learned orbits a central sun representing your level
- Concept Map — shows how your learned topics connect
- Learning DNA fingerprint

**Tech stack:**
Next.js 16 (App Router, Turbopack), Supabase, Vercel, Framer Motion. Mobile PWA with devicemotion shake-to-random-topic.

The 5-level structure was the original insight — most "explain it simply" tools give you one register and you're stuck with it. The level system lets you go deeper or pull back depending on what you already know.
