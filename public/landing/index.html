🍎 Black Holes — Level 1

A black hole is like a giant vacuum cleaner in space that sucks in everything nearby — even light can't escape! It forms when a very massive star collapses under its own weight.

🎓 Black Holes — Level 5

A black hole is a region of spacetime where the gravitational field is so intense that the escape velocity exceeds c. Described by the Schwarzschild metric, the event horizon marks the boundary beyond which causal contact is severed. Hawking radiation predicts thermal emission via quantum effects near the horizon.
